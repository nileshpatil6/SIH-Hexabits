### 1. Why It Currently Looks Flat & Template-Like

1. **Amateur 2D Flat Clipart:** Slides 2 and 5 use generic, cartoonish vector humans (undraw/freepik style). In an ISRO hackathon, this signals "student project" rather than mission-critical defense/disaster infrastructure.
2. **Slide 1 Looks Like a Government Form:** Half the cover is an unstyled metadata table, paired with an overused stock "brain-circuit" icon. There is zero visual drama or sense of innovation.
3. **Crude Technical Artifacts:** Slide 3’s phone mockup is a raw, wireframe-like border with plain text pasted inside. The pipeline flowchart below it uses basic, sharp-cornered flowchart boxes that look like a 2005 Visio diagram.
4. **Color & Tag Chaos:** Slide 3 dumps 16 multi-colored pills (orange, green, teal, navy) with no visual hierarchy. Slide 6 is a wall of plain blue text links. It overwhelms the eye without guiding it.
5. **No Sense of Physical Scale or Depth:** Every container has a flat fill and a hard border. There are no soft elevation shadows, glossy badges, or realistic textures to create visual layers.

### 2. Visual Direction: "Aero-Tactile Minimal"

* **Style Name:** **Aero-Tactile Minimal** (Precision engineering meets Apple/Linear light aesthetic).
* **Background:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a faint radial gradient in the top-right corner (`#F1F5F9` down to `#FFFFFF`, 40% opacity).
* **Card Treatment:** 
  * *Fill:* Solid `#FFFFFF` or frosted ice-tint `#F8FAFC`.
  * *Border:* 1px hairline stroke in `#E2E8F0`.
  * *Shadow:* Ultra-soft double shadow: `Y: 2px, Blur: 8px, Color: #0F172A @ 4%` + `Y: 10px, Blur: 24px, Color: #0F172A @ 4%`.
  * *Radius:* 12px rounded corners.
* **Color Usage Ratios:**
  * **65% Canvas:** White & crisp cool grey (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`).
  * **20% Deep Navy:** Structural text, primary titles, heavy data anchors (`#0A192F` / `#0F2744`).
  * **10% Signal Amber/Saffron:** High-contrast focal stats, active alerts, latency metrics (`#F97316`).
  * **5% Mesh Cyan/Emerald:** Verification badges, radio signals, latency drops (`#0284C7` / `#10B981`).
* **Typography Hierarchy (16:9 Slide Scale):**
  * *Slide Titles:* 26pt Bold, Navy (`#0A192F`).
  * *Hero Numbers/Metrics:* 36–40pt ExtraBold, Navy or Saffron.
  * *Card Headings:* 13–15pt SemiBold.
  * *Body Text:* 10–11pt Regular, Charcoal Slate (`#334155`).
  * *Micro-tags / Footnotes:* 8–9pt Medium, Slate (`#64748B`).
* **Signature Motif:** **The Radio-Pulse Node**—a subtle, concentric 3-ring hairline arc emitting from icons and data points to visually tie "Voice $\to$ Tiny Mesh Packet $\to$ Receiver."
